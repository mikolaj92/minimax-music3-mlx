Genre: high-energy J-pop fused with Polish highland folk. BPM: 148. Bright, youthful, powerful female lead vocal with festival cheer and occasional folk shouts of hej. Driving J-pop drums, sparkling synth leads, punchy electric bass, Polish highland fiddle, accordion stabs, clap-along folk choir. Major key, euphoric, danceable. Verse tight and rhythmic, pre-chorus lifts, chorus huge and anthemic with fiddle countermelody, bridge more folk-dance then explodes back. High energy throughout, night-festival atmosphere, no ballad, no lo-fi.
